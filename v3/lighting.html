Signs it's time to call

  • Dark rooms, kitchens or work areas
  • Upgrading to recessed, pendant or accent lighting
  • Adding exterior or landscape lighting
  • Fixtures that flicker or fail often

How it works

  1. Request an estimate or call 417-216-0102.
  2. Get a free estimate.
  3. We explain what's happening, why it matters, and your options.
  4. Permits and inspections: many electrical jobs need a permit and inspection from your city or county. Ask about it at your estimate.
  5. The work is done, then a final walkthrough. It's backed by a one-year labor warranty.

Timeline: it depends on scope, parts and inspections. Ask for one with your estimate.

What drives the cost

  • Number and type of fixtures
  • Ceiling access and height
  • New switching or dimmers
  • Exterior runs and weather-rated parts

Estimates are free, so you'll know the price before work starts.
